## Accessibility Issue or Topic

The complainant-authored text raises access to lift controls, common routes, and shared amenities for blind residents. It alleges that residential lift touch panels lack tactile feedback and Braille; that routes to the clubhouse, library, and swimming-pool area rely on stairs; and that common paths lack tactile guiding or warning features. These are party allegations, not findings about the site or conclusions about legal compliance.

The section-specific portal relief entries are preserved as Markdown source files without content edits. They request, among other remedies, accessible lift controls and amenity routes, interim assistance, and statutory relief. The user says the entries were submitted under the corresponding sections selected in the portal's “Relief Sought” step. The individual entries are linked below so readers can consult their full wording rather than treating this summary as a substitute.

A separate local hearing-preparation note describes navigation and safety difficulties as reported experience. The interim-relief entry requests temporary assistance and tactile route measures, among other relief. Filing of relief text does not establish that any requested measure was granted.

The MahaRERA order and roznama concern duplicate-complaint procedure. They do not adjudicate the access concerns. The order refers to an earlier complaint being listed according to seniority as of the hearing; the current status of that earlier matter is not established here.
