Relief Sought under Section 11(4)(a), 11(4)(b), and 11(4)(d):
1. Under Section 11(4)(a) & 11(4)(b) (Obligation to Hand Over Legally Compliant Common Areas): Direct the Promoter to discharge their statutory functions by delivering common areas, structures, and vertical transit systems that strictly comply with local development laws (Maharashtra UDCPR 2020, Chapter 9) and central civil rights laws (Section 44 of the RPWD Act, 2016). A builder cannot fulfill their duty to obtain a valid, legal Occupancy Certificate under Subsection (4)(b) while leaving the main infrastructure non-compliant and structurally inaccessible to visually impaired residents.
2. Under Section 11(4)(d) (Obligation to Provide and Maintain Functional Essential Services): Declare that vertical elevator transit and unhindered ingress to common amenities (Clubhouse, Library, and Swimming Pool) constitute "essential services" that the promoter is legally mandated to maintain for all allottees. Installing flat, non-tactile touch screens completely denies the Complainant the execution of this essential service.
3. Specific Orders Prayed For: Direct the promoter to fulfill their active duties under Section 11 by completing the physical infrastructure—specifically replacing the non-accessible capacitive touch screens with tactile, physical Braille button control panels across all 12 residential lifts, and providing accessible ramps to common recreation spaces, entirely at the promoter's own cost prior to final conveyance.
