Relief Sought under Section 61 (Imposition of Statutory Penalty for Comprehensive Contraventions):
1. Invocation of the Catch-All Corporate Penalty: Direct the Authority to invoke its penal powers under Section 61 against the Promoter (Majestique Landmarks / Majestique Empires LLP) for the continuous, ongoing contravention of the rules, regulations, and statutory mandates established under this Act—specifically Section 11(4) (failure to maintain essential transit services) and Section 14(1) (failure to adhere to lawful, barrier-free building specifications as mandated under Chapter 9 of the Maharashtra UDCPR 2020).
2. Assessment of Regulatory Fine Based on Estimated Project Cost: Order that because the Promoter has knowingly installed completely non-accessible, glass capacitive touch screens that exclude and endanger visually impaired residents, they be held liable to a statutory penalty under Section 61, extending up to five per cent (5%) of the total estimated cost of this real estate project.
3. Specific Orders Prayed For: Direct the Promoter to immediately avoid the full execution of this heavy corporate penalty by completely purging their statutory defaults—specifically by replacing the flat touch screens with physical, tactile Braille button panels across all 12 residential lifts entirely at their own cost within a strict, court-mandated timeline.
