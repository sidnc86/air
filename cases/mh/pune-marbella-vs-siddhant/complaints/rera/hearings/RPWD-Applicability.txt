* The Law: Section 3(1) states that no person with a disability shall be discriminated against on the ground of disability, unless it is a proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim. * Section 5: Right to Community Life
This section explicitly addresses where and how a person with a disability chooses to live, directly protecting them from being forced into segregated housing or facing residential restrictions.
* **Section 5(1):** Declares that persons with disabilities have an absolute **right to live in the community**.
* **Section 5(2):** Places a duty on the government to ensure they are integrated into the community and **are not forced to live in any particular or restrictive residential arrangement**.
