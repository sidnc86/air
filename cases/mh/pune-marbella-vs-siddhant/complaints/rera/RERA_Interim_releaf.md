Interim Relief Sought under Section 36 of the RERA Act, 2016:
Pending the final inquiry and resolution of the substantive structural defects under Sections 4, 11, and 14, the Complainant seeks the following explicit, non-transferable interim protections to mitigate severe daily safety hazards:
1. Deployment of Dedicated, Independent Lift Operators: Direct the Promoter to immediately recruit and deploy dedicated lift operators whose exclusive, single point of duty is the manual execution of touch-panel inputs across the affected residential elevators. To ensure reliable, immediate assistance without compromising general community safety, this responsibility shall not be diverted or offloaded onto the project’s existing gate security guards or building maintenance personnel.
2. Installation of Temporary Non-Visual Pathways: Direct the Promoter to immediately install high-contrast, non-destructive temporary tactile guiding mats connecting the main Wing D entrance lobby directly to the transit elevator bank. This provides immediate, basic orientation and hazard mitigation while the main structural dispute regarding the touch screens is pending inquiry.
3. Restraint on Occupancy Certificate (OC) Applications: Restrain the Promoter from applying for, utilizing, or processing any Part-Occupancy Certificate (Part-OC) or Final Occupancy Certificate for Wing D until a formal physical accessibility verification is executed at the site. This ensures the Promoter does not circumvent their statutory registration obligations under Section 4(2)(l)(E) and Section 11(4)(b).
4. Interim Funding & Penalty Escrow Clause: Direct that all costs associated with the interim lift operators and temporary tactile paths be borne entirely by the Promoter. If the Promoter fails to deploy these safety measures within seven (7) days of the order, a daily penal interest under Section 38(1) and Section 18(3) shall be levied against the Promoter's separate project bank account until full compliance is achieved.
