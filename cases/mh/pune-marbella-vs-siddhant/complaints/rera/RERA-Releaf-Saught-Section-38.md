Relief Sought under Section 38(1) (Imposition of Penalty and Prescribed Interest for Statutory Contraventions):
1. Imposition of Strict Financial Penalty for Wilful Non-Compliance: Exercise the powers vested under Section 38(1) to impose a substantial, exemplary financial penalty on the Promoter (Majestique Landmarks / Majestique Empires LLP) for the ongoing, flagrant contravention of their structural obligations. The promoter has knowingly installed flat, glass touch-panel elevator interfaces that completely exclude and endanger visually impaired residents, directly violating Maharashtra UDCPR 2020 (Chapter 9) and the RPWD Act, 2016 (Section 44).
2. Levy of Compounding Interest to Force Compliance: Direct the Promoter to pay penal interest at the prescribed MahaRERA rate under Section 38(1) for every day the structural accessibility defects continue to exist past the 30-day statutory notice period, thereby financially incentivizing immediate remediation.
3. Specific Orders Prayed For:
• Direct the Promoter to immediately purge the statutory contraventions by removing the inaccessible touch-screen panels and installing physical, accessible Braille button control panels across all 12 residential lifts entirely at their own cost.
• Direct that any penalty levied under Section 38(1) be explicitly tied to a strict, court-monitored timeline to prevent the developer from delaying the necessary modifications.
