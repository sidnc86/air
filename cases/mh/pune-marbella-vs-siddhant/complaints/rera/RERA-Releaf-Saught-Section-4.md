Relief Sought under Section 4(2)(d), 4(2)(e), and 4(2)(l)(E):
1. Declaration of Breach of Sworn Statutory Affidavit: Declare that the Promoter (Majestique Landmarks / Majestique Empires LLP) is in direct, material breach of their mandatory Form B Affidavit submitted under Section 4(2)(l)(E), wherein they swore under oath to secure all pending municipal approvals on time and in accordance with prevailing laws. By constructing common transit infrastructure that actively violates local municipal codes (Maharashtra UDCPR 2020, Chapter 9), the Promoter has compromised the legality of their project registration documents.
2. Rectification of Declared Project Specifications: Under Section 4(2)(d) and 4(2)(e) (which mandates the disclosure of true project specifications and development works), direct the Promoter to match their actual physical execution with the lawful standards required for residential high-rises. A project specification cannot be legally "completed" if it structurally excludes visually impaired allottees from safe, independent transit.
3. Specific Orders Prayed For: Direct the Promoter to immediately honor their statutory registration undertakings by purging the defaults in their Form B declaration. Specifically, order them to replace the glass touch screens with physical, tactile Braille button panels across all 12 residential lifts, ensuring the project aligns with the legal parameters promised during registration, entirely at the promoter's own cost.
