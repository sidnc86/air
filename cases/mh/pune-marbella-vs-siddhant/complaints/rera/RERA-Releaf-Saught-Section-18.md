Relief Sought under Section 18(1) and 18(3):
1. Under Section 18(3) (Compensation for Failure to Discharge Statutory Obligations): Hold the Promoter liable to pay direct financial compensation to the Complainant for failing to discharge their clear statutory obligations under the RERA Act, the Maharashtra UDCPR 2020 (Chapter 9), and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPWD) Act, 2016. The intentional installation of inaccessible flat touch screens and stairs-only amenities has caused a severe loss of utility, psychological distress, and a complete restriction of independent mobility within the common spaces.
2. Under Section 18(1) Proviso (Monthly Interest for Delayed Legal Possession): Declare that the physical hand-over of a flat served by non-compliant, illegal vertical transit infrastructure does not constitute valid or lawful "possession." Direct the Promoter to pay the Complainant interest for every month of delay at the prescribed MahaRERA rate (MCLR plus 2%) from the promised possession date until the date the infrastructure is made legally compliant and a valid Occupancy Certificate is secured.
3. Specific Orders Prayed For:
• Direct the Promoter to completely mitigate the financial and structural liability by funding and executing the replacement of the touch-screens with physical Braille/tactile button panels across all 12 residential lifts without any charge to the allottees.
• Alternatively, levy a strict daily or monthly financial penalty against the Promoter under Section 18(3) to be paid to the Complainant as ongoing compensation until the structural accessibility defects are verified as fully resolved.
