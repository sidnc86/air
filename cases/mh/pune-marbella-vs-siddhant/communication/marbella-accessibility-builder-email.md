**Subject:** Request for Universal Accessibility Compliance: Clubhouse, Lifts, and Walkways 

**Dear Majestique Marbella Builder Team,** 

I am writing to you as a resident of Wing D. Having lived here since May, I have been observing the progress of the final wings and the upcoming clubhouse with much hope. 

I would like to highlight a few challenges regarding the current design that affect the independent movement and safety of many residents, including senior citizens and those with disabilities. My intention is to share these observations now, while amenity construction is active, to ensure our society is inclusive and compliant with the latest standards. 

# **1. Clubhouse Access (Ramp Entry)** 

Currently, the clubhouse is only accessible via stairs. This creates a significant barrier for residents who use wheelchairs, parents with strollers, and our elderly neighbors with joint issues. 

- **Request:** Please ensure a ramp entry is integrated into the clubhouse before the official handover. To meet the **Harmonised Guidelines (2021)** , the ramp should adhere to the following technical standards: 

   - **Gradient:** A slope of **1:12** (maximum) is required, though **1:15** is preferred for easier manual wheelchair use. 

   - **Width:** A minimum clear width of **1200 mm** . 

   - **Surface:** Must be **non-slip** or even tactile to prevent skidding during monsoons. 

   - **Handrails:** Continuous circular handrails on both sides, mounted at two heights ( **700 mm and 900 mm** ) to assist both children and adults. 

   - **Landing:** A level landing of at least **1500 mm x 1500 mm** at every change of direction and at the top/bottom. 

# **2. Lift Operations (Braille & Audio)** 

The touch-panel lifts in Wings D, E, and F offer no tactile feedback for the visually impaired, and the lack of audio announcements makes them difficult to use independently. 

- **Recommendation:** To avoid the high cost of replacing entire panels, I have researched feasible alternatives. An **Auxiliary Braille Button Panel** can be wired into the existing system. This would provide physical buttons for those who need them without disrupting the current touch interface. 

- **Note:** I have even been in touch with **Kone** regarding mobile-app based operations as a modern workaround. However, a physical tactile option remains a fundamental requirement for universal access under the **RPWD Act** . 

# **3. Walkways and Transition Points** 

The pathways leading from the main gate to the buildings and society office currently lack tactile guiding tiles. These are essential for residents with low vision to navigate safely. 

- **Request:** Please include **Tactile Ground Surface Indicators (TGSI)** in the final landscaping. This includes: 

   - **Guiding Blocks:** (Linear bars) to indicate the correct path of travel. 

   - **Warning Blocks:** (Dotted studs) to indicate turns, steps, or entrances to the society office and building lobbies. 

# **Why this matters now:** 

Under the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPWD) Act, 2016** , these features are not optional; they are mandatory legal requirements for all residential complexes to receive a final **Occupancy Certificate (OC)** . As the work for Wings A, B, and C is nearing completion, integrating these features now will be far more cost-effective than retrofitting them later. 

We all want Majestique Marbella to be a model of modern, thoughtful living. I hope you will view these as necessary upgrades to ensure the safety and comfort of all your customers. 

I would be happy to discuss these technical specifications or the Kone alternatives further to help find the most efficient way forward.
