**Subject:** STATUTORY NON-COMPLIANCE: Legal Evidence & Handover Risks for Majestique Marbella 

# **Dear Committee Members,** 

As we approach the final handover of common amenities, I wish to present the **legal framework** that makes the current state of our clubhouse and lifts a liability for our society. 

Under the **Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) 2020** (Maharashtra's primary building law), any building constructed after 2020 **cannot legally be granted an Occupancy Certificate (OC)** if it lacks barrier-free access. 

# **Critical Legal Points to Note:** 

1. **Lifts (NBC 2016, Part 3, Section 13):** The mandate for audio announcements and Braille is **not** restricted to commercial buildings; it applies to all residential high-rises. 

2. **Ramps (UDCPR Regulation 33.1):** A clubhouse with stairs-only access is a direct violation of the mandatory provisions for "Physically Handicapped Persons." 

3. **Liability:** If the society accepts the handover "as-is," the cost of retrofitting (approx. ₹5–10 lakhs per lift as per the attached Kone quote) will eventually fall on the residents' maintenance funds. 

# **Attached for your reference:** 

- **UDCPR 2020 (Excerpts):** Regulation 33 specifies accessibility needs. 

- **Kone Quotation:** For the Audio Announcement System. 

- **Harmonised Guidelines (2021):** Outlining the 1:12 ramp gradient and tactile pathway requirements. 

I urge the committee to include these rectifications as a **pre-condition for handover acceptance** . Accepting a non-compliant project puts our final OC at risk and discriminates against our own elderly and disabled residents.
