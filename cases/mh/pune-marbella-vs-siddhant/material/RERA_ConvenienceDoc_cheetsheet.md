# Real Estate Violations 

1. **MahaRERA Form B (Declaration under Oath by the Promoter):** The Promoter has given a statutory undertaking via Form B to comply with all local municipal rules. By failing to install barrier-free infrastructure mandated by UDCPR 2020, the promoter is in direct breach of their sworn declaration to MahaRERA. 

2. **The Maharashtra Lift Act and Rules (Statutory Lift Clearance):** MahaRERA mandates a risk-free path to legal handover. Because the capacitive flat touchpanels render the lifts non-compliant under the Lift Inspector's structural benchmarks for public safety, the builder cannot secure clear, non-conditional statutory lift permits. 

3. **Real Estate Act 2016:** An amenity that cannot physically be accessed by a segment of allottees due to structural barriers (such as stairs-only configurations) is a complete failure to deliver the service in a legally functional state, constituting an operational defect under Section 11. 

4. **MahaRERA Order No. 57 (Mandatory Disclosure of Facilities):** This regulation forces every promoter to explicitly declare the exact nature of the common amenities and common areas in the Agreement for Sale. 

# Municiple And State Laws 

1. Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) 2020: Chapter 9 / Regulation 33.1 (Mandatory facilities for Persons with Disabilities for granting an Occupancy Certificate). 

# National Mandates 

1. National Building Code (NBC) 2016: Part 3, Section 13 (Mandatory audio announcements and Braille/tactile indicators in high-rise residential lifts). 

2. The Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPWD) Act, 2016: Section 44 (Mandatory accessibility standards for all public and private common-use buildings).
