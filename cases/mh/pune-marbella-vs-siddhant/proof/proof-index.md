# Proof Documentation Index

This index documents each piece of evidence in the proof folder, explaining its purpose and relevance to the RERA complaint and accessibility barriers identified in the Marbella case.

## Overview

The photos and PDFs in this folder serve as visual evidence of accessibility barriers and design features at Marbella, Pune (Pune Wing D and Wing E). They support the arguments in the complaint, relief-sought sections, and proposed lift-panel solution.

---

## Entry and Common Area Photos

### ClubHouse_Library_Swimming_Entry.jpeg
**Purpose:** Demonstrates the entry point accessibility to the clubhouse, library, and swimming pool facilities.  
**Relevance:** Entry accessibility is a foundational requirement under building codes. This photo shows the approach and entry design for common amenities.

### PodiumEntry.jpeg
**Purpose:** Documents the entry design and accessibility features (or barriers) at the main podium/reception area.  
**Relevance:** Main building entry and reception accessibility is critical for all residents and visitors, especially those with mobility disabilities.

## Lift Panel Documentation

### WingD-lift-panels.pdf
**Purpose:** PDF documentation of the current lift (elevator) panel layout in Wing D, including control button arrangement and current design.  
**Relevance:** Elevator panel accessibility (button labels, Braille, tactile indicators, voice announcements) is a key barrier documented in the complaint. This serves as baseline documentation for the existing condition.

### Elevator panel measurements.pdf
**Purpose:** Detailed measurements and dimensional specifications of the existing elevator panel.  
**Relevance:** Precise measurements are essential for designing the proposed Braille overlay and ensuring it meets accessibility standards without obstructing function or safety features.

## Summary

All proof materials in this folder are user-captured evidence from Marbella, Pune. They are referenced in:
- The complaint submissions to MahaRERA
- The relief-sought sections (particularly concerning lift accessibility and common area design)
- The proposed touch-panel Braille overlay solution (see `proposed-solution/`)

These materials remain unchanged and are included in the public repository to serve as concrete evidence for community members pursuing similar accessibility advocacy and building compliance cases.

**Note:** Images are user-captured; no modifications have been made to their content. PDFs are originals from the property documentation and complaint filing process.
