# Citation note — 2026-11-13

The documents in this case folder were filed or sent before this note and are kept as they were.

## NBC 2016 citation form

Filed documents refer to "NBC 2016, Part 3, Section 13". The correct form is **NBC 2016, Volume 1, Part 3, Clause 13 and Annex B**. The accessibility provisions are in Annex B. See [`02-NBC-2016-Accessibility-Clause-Map.md`](../../../mandates/national/02-NBC-2016-Accessibility-Clause-Map.md).

## Lift control heights

- Car control panel: 900–1100 mm above floor, with Braille and raised characters; touch panels not permitted (Annex B, B-6.4.5).
- Landing call button: 800–1000 mm above floor (Annex B, B-6.4.4).

A panel measured at 1.45 m is above both ranges.

## Official text

The full NBC 2016 Volume 1 is hosted by the [Uttarakhand Town and Country Planning Department](https://tcp.uk.gov.in/document/national-building-code-of-india-2016-vol-i-part-1/). The figures above and in the clause map were checked against it on 2026-11-13.
