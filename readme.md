This workspace is being prepared as a source-attributed resource about accessibility in Indian built environments. It is intended for disabled people, older people, families, practitioners, building operators, and others working toward more accessible homes and public places.

## Our Mission: Knowledge Through Cases

Real RERA complaints, appeals, and advocacy work are the foundation of this knowledge base. By sharing your case—your complaint documents, relief-sought submissions, and evidence—you help other disabled people, families, and advocates understand:
- How to file a RERA or building-code complaint.
- What barriers and violations look like in real buildings.
- What documentation and evidence matter.
- How long the process takes and what to expect.

**If you have a case to share,** read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for guidelines on privacy, attribution, and how to contribute. Your individual advocacy strengthens everyone's ability to fight for accessible buildings.

## Folder Guide

- [`mandates/`](mandates/README.md) contains candidate laws, rules, standards, and guidance **stored as markdown for LLM accessibility**. Large PDFs (63+ MB) have been extracted and converted to searchable markdown files with live government source URLs for verification and amendment tracking. Source records state what has and has not been verified. See [`legal-mandates-sources.md`](mandates/legal-mandates-sources.md) for:
  - ✅ Converted markdown mandates with extraction dates
  - 📋 Official government source URLs for compliance verification
  - 🔔 Community update-tracking framework (flag outdated material via GitHub issues)
  - 📊 Verification status of each mandate
- [`cases/`](cases/) contains real RERA complaints, appeals, relief-sought sections, and supporting evidence shared by community members. These materials are preserved as authored and are not modified. **[Help us build this knowledge base](#our-mission-knowledge-through-cases)—share your case to help others.**
- [guides/](guides/README.md) contains independently written procedural orientation, beginning with a preliminary Maharashtra RERA guide.
- [solutions/](solutions/touch-panel/README.md) contains practical ideas and references. A proposal is not evidence that a design meets a standard or works for every user.
- `templates/` contains blank contribution aids; templates do not authorize copying case material.

## Sources and Limits

**All material in this repository is under review.** Read [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md) for citation, verification, and rights-status conventions. 

A link being reachable does not mean its text, applicability, or reuse permission has been verified. This material is **not legal advice** and does not guarantee that a design or filing complies with applicable requirements.

**Do not rely on unverified legal citations, procedure steps, or compliance claims** without independently confirming them with current official sources. See [`legal-mandates-sources.md`](mandates/legal-mandates-sources.md) for verification status of each mandate.

The root [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) describe a limited license for original project-authored educational text. They do not license case material or third-party documents. See [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md) for publication and privacy boundaries.

## Contributing

Please read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) before proposing material. **Case contributions are especially welcome**—see the [Case Material Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md#case-material-guidelines-for-contributors) to share your RERA complaint, appeals, or advocacy documents.

The project does not yet provide private intake. Do not send sensitive records through public GitHub features unless you have consented to public sharing. If GitHub contribution channels are enabled later, only rights-cleared public material should be submitted there.

The project roadmap is in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). Nontechnical participation and a private review workflow remain future work; no upload form is available yet.
