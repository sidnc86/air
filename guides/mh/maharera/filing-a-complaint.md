# Maharashtra RERA Complaint: Preliminary Orientation

**Status:** Preliminary orientation only. This is not a complete filing guide or legal advice. Official entry points were checked on 2026-11-04; current form fields, fees, deadlines, eligibility, documents, and end-to-end portal steps have not been verified.

## Official starting points

1. Review the [MahaRERA home-buyers guidance](https://www.maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/guidance-home-buyers-0) and follow its current official links.
2. The [MahaRERA complaint portal entry](https://maharerait.maharashtra.gov.in/login/) is an official starting point. The current authenticated workflow and requirements have not been reviewed for this guide.
3. Before filing, confirm the applicable route, current forms, fee, limitation or deadline, required documents, and any project-specific requirements directly from current official material or a qualified adviser.

## Handling records safely

Keep original records private and unchanged. Do not post agreements, correspondence, payment records, identity details, or case filings in public issues or pull requests. The repository's `cases/` directory is not an upload destination and is outside this guide's scope.

This guide does not determine legal jurisdiction, assess a particular dispute, or promise an outcome. If an official source does not answer a procedural question, record it as unverified rather than filling the gap from memory. Further verification is needed before this can be treated as a step-by-step filing guide.
