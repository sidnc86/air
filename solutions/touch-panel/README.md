# Touch-Panel References

This folder contains illustrative lift touch-panel reference files and a partial Unicode Braille chart. The images and presentation are not a verified design specification, Braille transcription, tactile layout, or evidence of compliance. Their provenance and reuse rights should be recorded before public redistribution.

- [`braille-unicode-characters.md`](braille-unicode-characters.md): partial and unverified; includes a clearly U.S.-specific ADA comparison.
- [`Inner-Pannel.jpeg`](Inner-Pannel.jpeg), [`Panel-Layout.png`](Panel-Layout.png), and [`Panel-Layout.pptx`](Panel-Layout.pptx): created by the project maintainer, who reports taking the photos of the D Wing lift touch panel and preparing the layout. The concept is a metal or acrylic overlay with open slits aligned to the touch controls; the intervening grid is intended for Braille labels applied as stickers or embossed on the overlay. This documents creator-reported provenance and design intent, not verified dimensions, material suitability, touch interaction, tactile usability, safety, standards compliance, or external reuse permission.

Do not treat Unicode characters as a substitute for Braille-code selection, qualified transcription, physical dot dimensions, tactile testing, or installation requirements.
