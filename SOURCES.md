# Source and Attribution Rules

## Record each reference

Use the following fields when adding a source or citing an existing local document:

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Issuer or author | The body or person responsible for the source |
| Title | Exact publication or document title |
| Edition/date | Edition, notification date, revision, or `not stated` |
| Source URL | Official source where available; otherwise say how provenance is known |
| Date checked | Date the link or document was last checked, in `YYYY-MM-DD` form |
| Pinpoint | Exact section, clause, page, or table only when verified |
| Language | Language of the source |
| Link status | `checked`, `not checked`, `unavailable`, or equivalent explanation |
| Text status | `verified`, `not reviewed`, or the exact portion reviewed |
| Applicability status | `reviewed`, `not reviewed`, or a concise limitation |
| Rights status | `permission known`, `license identified`, `unknown`, or other evidence |
| Repository note | Independent summary/adaptation and any change from the source |

Do not treat one status as proof of another. A reachable URL does not mean that its text was read; a checked passage does not establish its legal applicability; public availability and attribution do not establish reuse permission.

## Attribution

Credit the issuer or author, identify the title and edition/date where available, link to the source, and include a pinpoint citation when verified. Clearly label independent summaries and translations. Do not copy substantial source text unless its reuse rights have been checked. Keep third-party documents' original files and bytes unchanged.

## Legal and technical claims

Distinguish statutes, regulations, standards, guidance, authority webpages, professional commentary, and personal experience. Separate what the source says from applicability analysis or recommendations. Use `not verified` rather than infer a clause, page, requirement, or compliance conclusion. Label foreign standards with their jurisdiction and do not present them as Indian requirements.

## Rights and publication

The root `LICENSE` is not a blanket grant over every repository file. The intended CC BY 4.0 scope is described in `LICENSE.md`. Case material, third-party sources, images, local PDFs, presentations, and archived web pages remain outside that scope unless separate permission or a suitable license is documented. When rights are unclear, record the uncertainty and do not publish or reproduce the material.
