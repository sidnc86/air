# Privacy and Publication Boundaries

## Phase 1 rule

The `cases/` directory contains filed or case-related material and is out of scope for Phase 1. Do not edit, rename, move, add, remove, copy, summarize, redact, or publish anything from that directory. Its wording and file bytes must remain as supplied.

Do not upload case records, correspondence, screenshots, payment records, agreements, personal information, or other evidence to a public repository, issue, pull request, discussion, or website. A filename or metadata record may itself reveal sensitive information; do not reproduce case file inventories in public documentation.

## Future use

Keeping a local copy does not grant permission to publish it. Any future case-derived summary or excerpt requires separate explicit approval, privacy review, and rights review. Such a derivative must be independently written and stored outside `cases/`; filed materials themselves remain unchanged. Review may require withholding material rather than editing the local original.

Before a public release, review the exact publication set, including Markdown, attachments, images, OCR, document metadata, and version history. Removing a file later may not remove copies from forks, clones, caches, or repository history. `.gitignore` alone is not a privacy control.

## Third-party material

Publicly accessible does not mean freely reusable. Do not reproduce or redistribute laws, standards, PDFs, images, newsletter material, or other third-party works unless the applicable permission or legal basis has been checked. Record rights status and source attribution separately; attribution is not permission.

## Contributions

Phase 1 does not provide a private intake service. Do not submit confidential or personal material through public GitHub features. A future submission workflow must define consent, access, retention, deletion, and review before collecting uploads.
