## How to read this map

- **Citation format:** cite as "NBC 2016, Part 3, Clause 13.5" or "NBC 2016, Part 3, Annex B, B-6.4.2". Earlier project files called this "Section 13". The correct reference is Part 3, Clause 13.
- **Force:** NBC uses "shall" for requirements and "should", "may" or "recommended" for guidance. This matters in a complaint. The Force column records the wording used: **Shall**, **Should** (includes "recommended" and "desirable"), or **Mixed** when a clause contains both.
- **★** marks clauses most relevant to residential buildings by private developers.
- **Legal status:** NBC is a model code. It becomes binding when adopted or referred to by law, for example in state development control regulations or rules made under the RPwD Act, 2016. Check how it applies in the specific state and project before asserting a violation.
- All dimensions are in millimetres unless stated.

## Clause 13: Scope and application

| Clause | Topic | Requirement (summary) | Force |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13.1.1 | Who is covered | Barrier-free access to, into and around buildings for older people and people with mobility, vision, hearing, coordination and other impairments, including temporary and age-related conditions. | — |
| 13.1.2 ★ | Which buildings | Applies to all buildings and facilities used by the public, and to **all public housing, including housing built by private developers**. Does not apply to private residences. | Shall |
| 13.1.3 | Extended meaning | References to disabled people also cover older people, children and people of short stature where relevant. | Shall |
| 13.2 | Definitions | 45 defined terms, including accessible route, accessible toilet, area of rescue assistance, LRV (light reflectance value), tactile ground surface indicators (TGSI) and universal design. | — |
| 13.3 | Design considerations | Design must account for the range of human abilities. Table 7 lists key issues: approach, entry, horizontal and vertical circulation, use of rooms, and evacuation. | — |
| 13.4 | Detailed provisions | The detailed requirements are in Annex B. Accessibility provisions in other Parts of the Code also apply. | Shall |
| 13.5 ★ | Minimum provisions | Apply Table 8 by occupancy. In every building: at least one accessible entrance (preferably the main one); all common areas and public facilities accessible; an accessible toilet in each toilet group. | Shall |
| 10.10 | Parking link | Designated accessible parking to be provided as per B-3 for the occupancies in Clause 13. | Shall |

### Table 8: Residential row ★

For public housing, including low-income housing and housing by private developers:

1. **Common areas:** all common and communal areas must be accessible. This includes the approach, entrances and exits, lifts, stairs, lobbies, clubhouse, religious facilities, parking, sports facilities, parks and recreation areas, and the sanitary facilities serving them.
2. **5% of dwellings** must be fully accessible, preferably on the ground floor.
3. **The other 95% of dwellings** need minimum universal design features: clear door widths for the main entrance, rooms and kitchen, and at least one toilet sized to B-9 and reached from the living or drawing room, so residents can adapt the home as needs change. Table 8 does not itself state the door width; B-5.3.2 sets 900 mm as the general minimum clear opening.

Table 8 also has rows for hotels, educational buildings, hospitals, assembly buildings, transit buildings, heritage sites, public toilets, restaurants, sports buildings and shops (for commercial scope later).
